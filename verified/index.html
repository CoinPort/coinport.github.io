Questions about your order

Why do you need my ID?

Australian anti-money-laundering law requires us to know who we trade with.

Do I have to verify again next time?

No, it's a one-time check. It also carries over if you ever trade with us at coinport.com.au: choose Sign in, tap "Forgot your password?" and enter the email you gave during your ID check. We'll email you a link to set a password, and once you're in you'll find you're already verified.

Is my information safe?

Your ID is checked by our identity verification partner and used only to meet our legal obligations. We don't use it for marketing, and we only share it where the law requires. See our privacy policy.

How long does the payment take?

Usually under a minute. We pay by PayID or Osko once your verification is complete.

My bank shows "Credit to account". Is that you?

Some banks show a general label in the transaction list. Open the transaction and you'll see it's from CoinPort, with the reference we gave you in the chat.

When should I release the crypto?

Only once the money is in your account. Check your banking app, not a screenshot or a message, then confirm in your order that you've received the payment.

The payment hasn't arrived. What do I do?

Message us in your order chat and don't release the crypto. Most payments land within a minute, but if something holds one up, we'll sort it out with you there.

Can I cancel the order?

Yes, as long as we haven't paid yet. Just message us in your order chat.

Who is CoinPort?

CoinPort Pty Ltd is an Australian business, operating since 2018 and registered with AUSTRAC as a virtual asset service provider.
