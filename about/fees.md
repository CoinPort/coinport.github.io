# Fees

CoinPort does not charge commissions or trading fees for crypto orders executed on the CoinPort exchange platform.

We aim to keep pricing simple and transparent. The price you see is the price you receive at execution.

CoinPort does not charge fees for AUD deposits or withdrawals made via the New Payments Platform (NPP).

When you withdraw cryptocurrency from CoinPort, we pay the blockchain network fee for you. The full amount you withdraw is sent, and nothing is deducted for the network.

When you send cryptocurrency to CoinPort from another wallet or exchange, that wallet or exchange may charge its own blockchain network fee. These fees are set by the underlying blockchain networks and the sending service, not CoinPort, and can vary with network conditions. For example, transaction fees on the Ethereum network have previously exceeded AUD $30 during periods of congestion.

## Spreads

CoinPort is the other party to every trade on the exchange. Instead of charging a fee, our buy price sits a small margin above the market price and our sell price a small margin below it. That margin is the spread. It is already included in the price shown in the order form, so nothing is added when your order executes.

Our spreads are typically between 0.3% and 2.5% either side of the market price, depending on the market. They can be wider when markets are moving quickly or trading is thin. The order form always shows the exact price before you confirm.
